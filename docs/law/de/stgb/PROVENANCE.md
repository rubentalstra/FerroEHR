# Strafgesetzbuch (StGB)

The criminal code, for one section: **§ 203**, the professional secrecy of physicians and their staff, which every access to a clinical record by a person outside the treatment context is measured against, and whose Abs. 3 and 4 (since 2017) govern disclosure to the persons and contractors who keep the systems running.
