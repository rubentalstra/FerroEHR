> B6-scope note: the chapter notes flag that the protocol tail (committal-header merge, `Last-Modified`, If-Match hardening, `OPTIONS /`) must land on **every** change-controlled resource, not just COMPOSITION. That verification is Phase 2/3 work — the requirements below (R2–R4, R7–R9, R13, R35) are the spec duties to check against each resource.
