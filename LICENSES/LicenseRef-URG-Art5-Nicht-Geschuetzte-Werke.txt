Swiss federal legislation: not protected by copyright (Art. 5 URG)
====================================================================

This identifier covers the Swiss federal acts and ordinances vendored under
`docs/law/ch/`, fetched from the Fedlex filestore of the Federal Chancellery
by `scripts/vendor/law-ch.sh`, in German and, where Fedlex publishes one at
the same consolidation, in the non-binding English translation. It is not a
licence and it is not a grant. It records that Swiss statute puts this
material outside copyright protection altogether, so no permission is needed
and none is claimed.

What the statute says
---------------------

Bundesgesetz über das Urheberrecht und verwandte Schutzrechte (Federal Act on
Copyright and Related Rights, URG, SR 231.1), Art. 5 "Nicht geschützte
Werke", quoted verbatim from the consolidation in force from 1 July 2025 at
https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1798_1798_1798/20250701/de, read
2026-09-13:

  1 Durch das Urheberrecht nicht geschützt sind:
    a. Gesetze, Verordnungen, völkerrechtliche Verträge und andere amtliche
       Erlasse;
    b. Zahlungsmittel;
    c. Entscheidungen, Protokolle und Berichte von Behörden und öffentlichen
       Verwaltungen;
    d. Patentschriften und veröffentlichte Patentgesuche.

  2 Ebenfalls nicht geschützt sind amtliche oder gesetzlich geforderte
    Sammlungen und Übersetzungen der Werke nach Absatz 1.

An unofficial English rendering, for a reader who needs one: copyright does
not protect acts, ordinances, international treaties and other official
enactments (paragraph 1 letter a), nor official or statutorily required
collections and translations of them (paragraph 2). The German text above is
the authoritative one; this sentence is a convenience and carries no legal
weight.

How it applies to this repository
---------------------------------

Every German file this identifier covers is one of the things Art. 5 Abs. 1
lit. a names: a federal act or an ordinance of the Federal Council or a
department, published in the Classified Compilation (Systematische
Sammlung des Bundesrechts) on Fedlex. Every English file is a translation of
one of them published by the same authority, which Art. 5 Abs. 2 puts in the
same place. Nothing here is protected by copyright, so redistributing it
verbatim needs no permission and grants none.

The English translations bind nobody, and Fedlex says so on each of them:
"English is not an official language of the Swiss Confederation. This
translation is provided for information purposes only and has no legal
force." German, French and Italian are the authentic languages; the German
text is the one vendored as the reference, and each act's `PROVENANCE.md`
points at the French and Italian consolidations on Fedlex.

What is vendored is the publisher's own HTML for a named consolidation and
language, byte for byte, with a SHA-256 recorded beside it. Each act's
`PROVENANCE.md` names the SR number, the ELI, the consolidation date and the
fetch, so a reader can re-derive the same document from the source rather
than trusting this copy.

The Fedlex web application (its styling, scripts and site chrome) is the
Federal Chancellery's and is not vendored: the fetch source is the filestore
document alone, which carries the act and its own minimal markup.
