Dutch legislation: no copyright subsists (Auteurswet Art. 11)
=============================================================

This identifier covers the Dutch acts and decrees vendored under
`docs/law/nl/`, fetched from wetten.overheid.nl by `scripts/vendor/law-nl.sh`.
It is not a licence and it is not a grant. It records that Dutch statute puts
this material outside copyright altogether, so no permission is needed and none
is claimed.

What the statute says
---------------------

Auteurswet (Copyright Act 1912, BWBR0001886), Article 11, quoted verbatim from
https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0001886/2026-01-01, read 2026-09-12:

  Er bestaat geen auteursrecht op wetten, besluiten en verordeningen, door de
  openbare macht uitgevaardigd, noch op rechterlijke uitspraken en
  administratieve beslissingen.

An unofficial English rendering, for a reader who needs one: no copyright
subsists in laws, decrees and ordinances issued by the public authority, nor in
judicial rulings and administrative decisions. The Dutch text above is the
authoritative one; this sentence is a convenience and carries no legal weight.

How it applies to this repository
---------------------------------

Every file this identifier covers is one of the things Article 11 names: an act
of parliament, a decree, or a ministerial order, issued by the Dutch public
authority and published in the official consolidated database at
wetten.overheid.nl. No copyright subsists in them, so redistributing them
verbatim needs no permission and grants none.

What is vendored is the publisher's own HTML for a named consolidation date,
byte for byte, with a SHA-256 recorded beside it. Each act's `PROVENANCE.md`
names the BWB identifier, the consolidation and the fetch, so a reader can
re-derive the same document from the source rather than trusting this copy.

The presentation layer of wetten.overheid.nl (its styling and site chrome, which
the served HTML carries along with the act) is the publisher's; nothing here
reuses it as a design, and none of it is served or rendered by this project.

The NEN standards cited by the same compliance pages are NOT covered by this
identifier and are NOT in the tree: NEN 7510-1:2024, NEN 7510-2:2024,
NEN 7512:2022 and NEN 7513:2018 are sold under copyright by NEN. Article 11
does not reach them, because a NEN standard is not an act of the public
authority even where legislation refers to it. Their directories under
`docs/law/nl/` carry a record and no text.
